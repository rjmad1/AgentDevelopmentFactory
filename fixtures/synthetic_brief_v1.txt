Synthetic Business Workflow Brief: Inbound Partner Inquiry Triage

Context:
A high-growth B2B fintech company receives incoming inquiries via an unauthenticated partner webhook.
Inquiries vary across technical integration queries, billing tier disputes, and emergency outage reports.

Intended Agent Behavior:
1. Parse incoming partner payload and categorize into Billing, Integration, or Outage.
2. If Billing, extract customer account identifier and route to partner tier queues.
3. High priority billing issues must be flagged with SLA response thresholds.
4. Outages must trigger emergency on-call alerting.

Operational Boundaries & Constraints:
- No automated database writes or financial refunds.
- Unrecognized message formats must NOT be silently dropped or guessed.
- Tools: Mock CRM lookup tool and Mock Pager tool only. No live external network connectivity.
